What are Crystals?

Crystals are naturally occurring minerals formed over thousands to millions of years through geological processes. Each crystal has a unique structure and vibrational energy.

Why Use Crystals?

Crystals are often used for healing, meditation, and emotional balance. Many believe they help reduce stress, improve focus, and attract positive energy.
